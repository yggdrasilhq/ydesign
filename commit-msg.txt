Pre-populate the shelf, restore the reading place, probe the surface

Owner direction 2026-09-07: ydesign rows read as laggingly lazy to
populate. Diagnosis, from the surface contract plus new app-side probes:

- The GUI refetches the rail only on the document_version EDGE, observed
  on the next heartbeat declare (<=4s) or ping (~2.5s). ydesign's action
  replies carried neither a schema nor refetch_document, so every shelf
  click (group expand, chapter open) sat through that wait. Replies now
  carry the posting pane's schema plus refetch_document: the shelf and
  the page repaint in one loopback round trip.
- Only the yggui group opened expanded at cold start. Every book of the
  mode now opens expanded (View::expanded_all); collapsing a group is
  the reader's gesture. A mode switch repopulates the whole shelf too.
- Nothing survived a restart. The view now persists to
  ~/.config/ydesign/state.json (atomic temp+rename) on every accepted
  action and restores at boot, re-validated against the current shelf;
  stale ids fall back to the mode home. An explicit --mode still wins.
- One unavailable registered project (a path that exists only on another
  fleet host) hard-exited the whole app at startup. Per-project and
  per-file failures now skip with a warning; config-level breakage
  (bad JSON, duplicate ids) is still fatal, and an escaping notebook is
  never served.
- ytrace probes (app "ydesign"): cold-start phases, declare heartbeats,
  pane fetch and action spans, so the next populate complaint is
  measured from records instead of argued from code.
